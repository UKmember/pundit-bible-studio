Phone alerts

Get a notification on this phone for goals, half-time, full-time and when a post is due. On iPhone this works once the Studio is on your home screen.

Teams & competitions

Alerts and automatic posts are only for games involving these teams (one per line; part of the name is fine).

Automatic match content

Drafts appear in Posts (orange in the calendar) for your watched teams. Nothing is posted without you.

Posting slots

The times your posts go into on the calendar (24-hour, comma separated).
