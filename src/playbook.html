Rules that move the numbers

Reply for the first hour

Answer the first 15–20 comments. Replies count as comments and keep the post climbing.

One question per post

A/B, a score or a name beats "thoughts?".

Links in the first comment

Upload images natively. Source links and PromptWise go in a pinned first comment.

No engagement bait

Facebook shows fewer people posts that say "like if", "share if", "tag 3 mates" or "type YES".

Careful with agency photos

Photos from Sky, BBC or clubs are usually Getty/PA. Wikimedia Commons photos are free to use with a credit.

Review every Sunday

Sort posts by comments in Meta Business Suite. Post more of the top two formats.
