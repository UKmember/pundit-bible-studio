Pundit Bible Studio: privacy policy

Pundit Bible Studio is a private tool used only by the owner of the Pundit Bible social media pages to plan posts and see their own channel statistics. It has no public sign-up.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, it reads the owner's own YouTube Analytics (including estimated revenue) and the owner's own Facebook Page insights (including monetisation earnings). It only reads; it never posts to YouTube or Facebook, and it never accesses anyone else's account.

How the data is used and stored

The figures are shown only to the owner, inside the Studio. They are stored in the owner's private database (Supabase), and the sign-in keys are kept in a private table that the app itself cannot read. Nothing is sold or shared with anyone.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from the Studio (Earnings → Connections), from your Google Account (Security → Third-party access) or from Facebook (Settings → Business integrations).

Contact

Questions: contact the Pundit Bible page owner through the Pundit Bible Facebook page.
