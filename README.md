# Pundit Bible Studio

Your own copy of the Pundit Bible content studio: posts, breaking news, match day, publishing packs, video tools.

**Start here: [SETUP.md](SETUP.md)**

- `src/` – the app itself
- `web/` – login, database connection, iPhone app files and icons
- `supabase/` – database set-up and the Claude functions
- `jobs/` + `.github/workflows/` – set-up, deploy, morning news posts and breaking news scans
- `data/` – your posts brought across from the Claude version
